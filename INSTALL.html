Part 2 · Google Sheet: readable layout (10 min)

  1. Open your responses Google Sheet → top menu Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Click inside the code, press Ctrl+A, then Delete, so the editor is empty.
  3. Copy Block 2 below, paste it in, and press Ctrl+S to save.
  4. Top right: Deploy → Manage deployments → click the pencil (Edit) → Version: New version → Deploy.
Do not click "New deployment". It creates a new web address, and the survey would keep sending answers to the old one.
  1. If Google asks to authorise: Review permissions → your account → Advanced → Go to (project name) → Allow. It's your own script editing your own Sheet.
  2. Go back to the Sheet tab and reload the page. After a few seconds a Survey menu appears next to Help.
  3. Click Survey → Rebuild "All responses" from raw data. Authorise again if asked, then run it once more. A popup says "Rebuilt 4 responses."
  4. Open the All responses tab. Find the test row (Department is sfsd), scroll to its last column Response ID, and copy that ID.
  5. Open the Excluded tab and paste the ID in column A, row 2. Type test in column B.
  6. Click Survey → Refresh summary. The Summary tab now counts 3 people.
  7. Share (top right) → General access → Restricted. Add your teammate by email. The Sheet holds emails, so it shouldn't be open to anyone with the link.
